A little about me

Hi, I am Dr. NAM

I am just a chemistry educator and STEM enthusiast who loves sharing. What makes me happiest is when someone sees chemistry the way I do: fun and intriguing. I teach at the Faculty of Pharmacy, UiTM Bertam, and most of what is here began as small things I made for my students. I hope you enjoy them, and keep learning along with me.

Questions

Quick answers

Is this only for students?

No. It is for learners of all ages: school pupils, university students, lecturers, working people and curious minds. Learning has no end date.

Do I need to log in or pay?

No. Everything is free to open. Some hubs let you sign in to save your progress.

Which one should I start with?

UiTM students: open the study hub for your course (PHD115 or PHD125). Everyone else: start with Chemistry Around You.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. The pages work on phones, tablets and laptops. A few games work best on a laptop with a webcam.

Is it in Bahasa Malaysia or English?

Most hubs use English. The Virtual Secret Lab and some STEM activities have Bahasa Malaysia too.

Keep learning, keep growing

Start from wherever you are. Pick anything that sparks your curiosity, and come back any time to learn a little more.
