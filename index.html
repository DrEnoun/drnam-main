Your lecturer

Hi, I am Dr. NAM

I teach chemistry at the Faculty of Pharmacy, UiTM Bertam. My goal is simple: bring everyday life into simple science, explained. Everything here is made for my students first, and shared with everyone.

Questions

Quick answers

Do I need to log in or pay?

No. Everything is free to open. Some hubs let you sign in to save your progress.

Which one should I start with?

UiTM students: open the study hub for your course (PHD115 or PHD125). Everyone else: start with Chemistry Around You.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. The pages work on phones, tablets and laptops. A few games work best on a laptop with a webcam.

Is it in Bahasa Malaysia or English?

Most hubs use English. The Virtual Secret Lab and some STEM activities have Bahasa Malaysia too.

Ready to start?

Pick a hub and spend ten minutes. That is enough to begin.
